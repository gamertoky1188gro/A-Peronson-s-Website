# Getting started

## Account types

| Account | For | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buyer** | Brands, importers, retailers (local or international) | Post buyer requests, find factories, sign contracts |
| **Factory** | Garment and textile manufacturers | Show products and capacity, answer requests, accept buying-house connections |
| **Buying House** | Sourcing intermediaries | Manage buyer requests for clients, work with a team of agents and partner factories |
| **Agent** | Team member of a factory or buying house | Created by the company in Member Management; works conversations assigned to them |

Owner and Admin are GarTexHub staff roles; you cannot sign up as one.

## Create an account

1. Open **Sign up** (`/signup`) and choose Buyer, Factory or Buying House.
2. Enter your name, organization name, email and a password. The password needs **8+ characters,
   a letter and a number**. "Passwords do not match" only appears once you type the confirmation.
3. Factories and buying houses can add **employee count** and **production capacity** (amount, unit and
   period, e.g. 50,000 pieces / month). These appear on your profile card.
4. Submit. You go straight to onboarding.

## Onboarding

Onboarding (`/onboarding`) collects your country, industry (Garments or Textile) and categories.
The last step, **certifications** (for example ISO 9001, WRAP, amfori BSCI, OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS), is optional — choose *Skip*
and add them later from your profile.

## Signing in

- **Email + password** on `/login`. Choose how long to stay signed in (12 hours up to 30 days).
- **Passkey** (fingerprint, face or device PIN) once you have added one under Settings → Security.
- **Forgot password?** on the login page opens `/forgot-password`. Enter your email; if an account
  exists, a reset link is sent. The page shows the same message either way, so it never reveals
  whether an email is registered.
- The link works **once** and expires after **30 minutes**. Choosing a new password signs out every
  other device.

## Find your way around

- **Main feed** (`/feed`) — posts, products and buyer requests from the network.
- **Search** (`/search`) — companies, products and requests with filters.
- **Notifications** (`/notifications`) — clicking one opens the exact post or item it is about.
- **Help** (`/help`) — FAQs and how-tos; the assistant answers common questions without signing in.
