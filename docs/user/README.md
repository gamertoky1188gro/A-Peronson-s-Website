# GarTexHub user guide

GarTexHub is a B2B sourcing marketplace for the garments and textile industry. Buyers, factories and
buying houses find each other, post requirements and products, talk in real time, and agree contracts.

| Guide | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Getting started](getting-started.md) | Account types, sign up, onboarding, signing in, passkeys, forgotten password |
| [Profiles & verification](profiles-and-verification.md) | Your company profile, what others see, the verified badge and its documents |
| [Sourcing](sourcing.md) | Feed, search, buyer requests, products, notifications, partner network |
| [Messaging & calls](messaging-and-calls.md) | Inbox and message requests, the buying-house conversation lock, audio/video calls |
| [Contracts & billing](contracts-and-billing.md) | Contracts and the Legal Vault, signing, Free vs Premium, wallet and coupons |
| [Account & security](account-and-security.md) | Settings, Owner Dashboard, team members, theme, sign-in security, active sessions, the assistant |

Need help that isn't here? Open **Help** (`/help`) in the app, ask the assistant (the round button
at the bottom corner), or email gartexhubsupport@gmail.com.

> GarTexHub does not process payments between trading partners. It handles discovery,
> communication and contracts. See [Contracts & billing](contracts-and-billing.md).
