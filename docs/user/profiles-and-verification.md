# Profiles & verification

## Your profile

Every company has a public profile: `/factory/:id`, `/buying-house/:id` or `/buyer/:id`.
The header card shows:

- **Organization** — your company name (not your personal name).
- **Industry** — *Garments Industry* or *Textile Industry*.
- **Employees** and **production capacity** (factories and buying houses), if you entered them.
- Country, categories, certifications, the verified badge, and your posts and products.

Signed-in members can open other companies' profiles, follow them, message them and see their ratings.

## Changing your organization name

The organization name identifies you to trading partners, so it can't be changed casually:

- **Not verified:** you can rename it, then it is locked for **90 days**.
- **Verified:** the name changes through verification — re-upload your trade license and documents.

## The verified badge

Verification is **document-based** and **approved by GarTexHub staff**; it cannot be bought without
documents. It is a monthly subscription and must stay current.

Public standards: **Help → Verification** (`/help#verification`). To apply, choose **Get verified**,
sign in, and upload the documents for your account type on `/verification`:

| Account | Documents |
| --- | --- |
| Factory | Company Registration, Trade License, TIN, Authorized Person NID, Company Bank Proof, ERC (Export Registration Certificate) |
| Buying House | Company Registration, Trade License, TIN, Authorized Person NID, Company Bank Proof |
| International buyer (EU / USA) | Business Registration, VAT (EU) or EIN (USA), EORI (EU) or IOR (USA), Bank Proof |

**Price:** US $1.99 for the first month, then $6.99 per month, paid from your GarTexHub
[wallet](contracts-and-billing.md#wallet-and-coupons). Accounts given a free verification window pay nothing
until it ends. The price is shown on the verification page before you confirm.

After approval you get a notification and the badge appears on your profile, posts and search results.
An expired license or unpaid renewal can remove the badge.
