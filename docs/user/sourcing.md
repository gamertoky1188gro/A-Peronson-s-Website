# Sourcing: feed, search, requests and products

## Main feed (`/feed`)

Posts, products and buyer requests from the network. You can react to, comment on, share and
report posts. **Sharing** a post gives a link to `/share/<type>/<id>`, a page that
shows that single item (it also works for people who are not signed in).

Your own posts are managed in **Feed management** (`/feed/manage`). Each post has a **Boost** button
that opens Boost details — see [Contracts & billing](contracts-and-billing.md#boosts).

## Search (`/search`)

Search companies, products and buyer requests. Filters include industry, category, country,
verified-only, MOQ and price range, lead time, certifications, fabric type and GSM, capacity, sample
availability and Incoterms/payment terms.

## Buyer requests (`/buyer-requests`)

- **Buyers** post a request: product, category, quantity, target price, timeline, and details.
- **Factories and buying houses** browse and search open requests, and contact the buyer.
- Each request shows **smart matches** — suppliers whose categories and capacity fit it.
- Buying houses can assign a request to one of their agents.

## Products (`/product-management`)

Factories and buying houses (and their agents) publish products with images or video, category,
MOQ and lead time. Products appear in the feed, in search, and on the company profile.

## Partner network (`/partner-network`)

Buying houses send connection requests to factories; the factory accepts or rejects them. Connected
partners are listed here and can be worked with as a supply network.

## Notifications (`/notifications`)

New messages, requests, connection requests, verification results, contract updates and more.
Clicking a notification opens the **exact** post, request or item it refers to. Turn notification
types on or off in **Settings → Notifications**.
