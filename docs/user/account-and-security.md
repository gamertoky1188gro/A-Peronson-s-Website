# Account, settings & security

## Settings (`/settings`)

Tabs, in order: **My Profile**, **Verification**, then the account settings **Notifications**,
**Security** and **Theme** at the bottom.

- **My Profile** — company details, logo, categories, certifications, employees and capacity. The
  organization name has rename rules — see [Profiles & verification](profiles-and-verification.md#changing-your-organization-name).
- **Notifications** — turn each notification type on or off.
- **Theme** — light / dark / system, and a colour theme. The default theme uses the GarTexHub logo colours.

## Owner Dashboard (`/owner`)

The control centre for factory and buying-house accounts. Everything company-wide lives here (and
only here): Dashboard home, Buyer requests, Chats, Partner network, **Leads (CRM)**, **Member
management** (add agents and set their permissions), **Contracts vault**, Insights & analytics,
Verification, **Subscription & Billing**, **Chatbot & Auto-reply** (turn the AI auto-reply on or off
and tune it), Assistant, **Branding**, and Settings.

## Security (Settings → Security)

- **Change password** — 8+ characters with a letter and a number.
- **Passkeys** — add fingerprint/face/device-PIN sign-in, and remove passkeys you no longer use.
- **Active sessions** — every device signed in to your account, with device, IP address and last
  activity. The current device is marked. **Sign out** any other session, or **sign out all other
  sessions** at once; that device is signed out on its next request.
- **Signing out** on a device ends that session on the server, not just in the browser.
- **Forgot your password?** Use the link on the login page — see [Getting started](getting-started.md#signing-in).
  Resetting your password signs out every other session.

## The assistant

The round button at the bottom corner opens the GarTexHub assistant. It answers common questions
(account types, verification, pricing, messaging, contracts, password help) in **English or Bangla**,
even when you are not signed in. Signed-in members on supported plans also get AI answers. The
assistant explains and navigates; it does not negotiate or make decisions for you. For anything it
can't answer it points you to Help or support.
