# Messaging & calls

## Inbox and message requests (`/chat`)

- Messages from **verified** companies go straight to your inbox.
- Messages from **unverified** accounts land in **Message Requests** first; accept one to move the
  conversation to your inbox.
- Chats support text and file/image attachments (with preview), and show who is online.

## Buying-house conversation lock

When an agent of a buying house or factory starts a conversation, it is **assigned to that agent**.
Other agents of the same company cannot message in that thread unless permission is granted. This
prevents two team members from negotiating with the same buyer at once.

## Audio & video calls (`/call`)

- Start an audio or video call from a chat, without leaving the conversation, or schedule one ahead.
- Calls may be recorded for security and compliance; **everyone is notified before recording starts**.
- A completed video call between buyer and factory is required before either side can sign a
  contract with the other (see [Contracts & billing](contracts-and-billing.md)).

## Staying connected

If your connection drops, a short **"Unstable connection"** notice appears and disappears by itself
once you are back online.
