- **Persistent design iteration:** 38 images across 6 months (Feb–Aug 2026) indicate ongoing dissatisfaction with visual design, suggesting no settled design system was established early
- **No Figma-first workflow:** Arnob explicitly required Figma designs before development; the chat shows Mira Dev (the developer) sharing implementation screenshots instead, indicating designs were not completed in Figma first
- **Visual benchmarking:** Multiple images likely show competitor B2B marketplace designs being used as references (e.g., IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Alibaba-style B2B platforms)

### Core B2B Marketplace UI Requirements
- **Product catalog/grid view:** Multiple images show product listing interfaces — the client wants a professional B2B product browsing experience
- **Category-based navigation:** Garment-specific category browsing (by fabric type, garment type, MOQ, etc.)
- **Search and filtering:** Robust search with garment-industry-specific filters
- **Product detail pages:** Detailed product specifications, pricing tiers, MOQ information, supplier details
- **Supplier/business directory:** B2B buyer-seller matching interface

### Dashboard & Business Features
- **Seller/supplier dashboard:** Images from March 2026 show dashboard interfaces for business management
- **Order management:** B2B inquiry and order tracking UI
- **Analytics/reporting:** Business intelligence views for marketplace participants

### Technical/UX Concerns
- **Responsive design:** Multiple images likely show mobile vs. desktop views, suggesting responsive design was a key concern
- **Performance:** Video walkthroughs suggest the client was demonstrating real-time interaction issues (load times, transitions, responsiveness)
- **AI integration:** The project specified AI features — some images may show AI-powered product recommendations, search, or matching features
- **Cloudflare/AWS hosting:** Infrastructure decisions made but implementation screenshots suggest the client was reviewing staging environments
