## Detailed Diff Analysis

The `ratingsService` manages three collections: ratings, milestones, and feedback requests. Milestones are recorded by other services (call session end, partner connection). When both qualifying milestones (`contract_signed` and `communication_completed`) are met for a profile-counterparty pair, a feedback request notification is created. The rating summary computes average score, recent average, distribution breakdown, and reliability metrics (confidence level, verified counterparty ratio, qualified interaction ratio). Profile pages fetch real ratings from the API.
