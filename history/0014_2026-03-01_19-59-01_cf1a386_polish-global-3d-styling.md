## Custom Title

**Refine 3D Card and Button Shadows, Focus Rings, and Dark Mode Glows**

## High-Level Summary

A focused polish pass on `src/App.css` that refines the 3D styling system. Adds box-shadow with inset highlights to cards, buttons, inputs, images, and tables. Significantly enhances dark mode with layered neon glow effects (cyan + purple) on hover. Focus rings get a sky-blue glow. Border radius increased from 8px to 10px.

## Key CSS Enhancements

- **Cards/surfaces**: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.55)` highlight + soft drop shadow
- **Buttons**: Same treatment with stronger shadow, hover lift (translateY(-1px))
- **Inputs**: Inset shadow with focus ring (sky-blue glow)
- **Images/videos**: 12px radius with shadow
- **Dark mode**: Cyan/purple composite glow on buttons and surfaces, intensified on hover
- **Tables**: border-color set to CSS variable

## Why

To polish the 3D dark theme and make it production-ready. The added neon glows in dark mode create a distinctive "cyberpunk" aesthetic.
