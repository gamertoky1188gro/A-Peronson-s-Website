## Custom Title

Harden WebAuthn passkey option generation

## High-Level Summary

Enhanced the passkey service to include additional WebAuthn parameters: user verification preference, authenticator attachment, attestation type, and a challenge timeout. This ensures more reliable and secure credential creation.

## File-by-File Breakdown

- **server/services/passkeyService.js** — Added `userVerification: 'preferred'`, `authenticatorSelection` with `residentKey: 'preferred'`, `attestation: 'none'`, timeout of 60000ms, and excludedCredentials from existing passkeys

## Detailed Diff Analysis

Added WebAuthn RP options configuration:

- `rpName: 'GarTexHub'`
- `userVerification: 'preferred'`
- `authenticatorSelection: { residentKey: 'preferred', requireResidentKey: false }`
- `attestation: 'none'`
- `timeout: 60000`
- `excludeCredentials` built from stored passkeys to prevent duplicate registrations

## Why This Change

Without these options, passkey registration could produce credentials that don't work cross-device or fail on certain authenticators.

Yes. These are standard WebAuthn best practices.
