## Custom Title

Add Account-Age Boost Ranking with Abuse Safeguards

## High-Level Summary

Rewrites the feed ranking algorithm to include an account-age boost multiplier for new accounts, with anti-abuse safeguards. New accounts get up to 1.35x boost for the first 30 days that decays over the next 60 days. The boost only applies when the account has sufficient profile completeness, verified contact, and positive activity quality.

## File-by-File Breakdown

- **server/services/feedService.js** (+161/-4): Complete rewrite of the feed ranking algorithm with boosted scoring, profile completeness checks, activity quality computation, and anti-abuse eligibility.

## Detailed Diff Analysis

The feed service now computes a `ranking_score` for each feed item: `recencyScore * boostMultiplier`. The boost multiplier is 1.35 for accounts ≤30 days old, linearly decays to 1 over the next 60 days. Anti-abuse checks ensure boost only activates when: profile completeness ≥50% (checked across 9 fields), contact is verified, and activity quality ≥0.4 (positive interactions / total interactions). The ranking results are logged for observability.
