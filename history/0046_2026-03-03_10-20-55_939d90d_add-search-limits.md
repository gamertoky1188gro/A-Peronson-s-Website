## Custom Title

Add Plan-Based Search Limits, Premium Filter Gating, and Quota UX

## High-Level Summary

Introduces a search access control system with daily usage quotas per plan (free vs. premium) and premium-gated advanced filters. Backend consumption tracking ensures users don't exceed their plan limits. The frontend respects these limits, displays remaining quota, and disables premium filters for free users.

## Detailed Diff Analysis

The `searchAccessConfig` defines basic filters (`q`, `category`) and advanced filters (`primary`, `moqRange`, `country`, `verifiedOnly`, `orgType`). Free users get 25 daily searches for requirements and products; premium gets 400. Advanced filters return HTTP 403 with `upgrade_required` codes when used on free plans. The quota system persists usage counters per user/action/date in JSON. The SearchResults page detects the user's plan, shows premium filters as disabled, and displays remaining daily quota.

## Why This Change May Have Been Needed

To monetize the search feature by gating advanced filters and limiting free-tier usage, encouraging premium upgrades.
