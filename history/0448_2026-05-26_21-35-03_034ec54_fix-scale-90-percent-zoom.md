## Why This Change

The page content was slightly wider than the viewport on some screen sizes, causing horizontal scrollbars. Scaling to 90% makes everything fit comfortably.

## Was It Useful

Yes — quick fix for horizontal overflow issues. However, `zoom` is a non-standard CSS property.

## Impact Analysis

**Medium.** Affects entire app layout. `zoom` works in Chromium/Firefox but is non-standard (CSS `transform: scale()` would be the standard alternative).
